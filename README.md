# The Mind Within

> An immersive VR "Day-in-the-Life" empathy experience simulating Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) and Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) in a localized Singapore setting.

Developed as a Final Year Capstone Project by Immersive Media students.

---

## Overview

**The Mind Within** is a narrative-driven virtual reality experience created to build empathy and raise awareness for invisible mental health struggles. By stepping directly into the shoes of Bryan, a young polytechnic student in Singapore, players experience firsthand how OCD and GAD compound across a regular day.

---

## Narrative & Gameplay Flow

* **Stage 1: HDB Bedroom (OCD & The Need for Control)**
  * Set in a localized Singapore HDB bedroom.
  * Features physical organization tasks, socket-based item placement, and custom "doubt" loops simulating compulsive routines.
* **Stage 2: Concert Hallway (GAD & Avoidance)**
  * Crowd-dodging mechanics and dynamic NPC proximity triggers.
  * Real-time anxiety meter management, safe corner alcoves, and positive thought pickups.
* **Stage 3: Rooftop Afterparty (Social Overload & Climax)**
  * Timed branching dialogue choices with World Space UI.
  * Dynamic intrusive thought bubbles that escalate in speed and visual clutter.
  * Sensory panic climax (URP post-processing, vignette pulsing, heartbeat audio) transitioning to a solid black screen featuring Singapore youth mental health statistics and crisis helplines.

## Tech Stack & Tools

* **Engine:** Unity 6 (Universal Render Pipeline - URP)
* **VR Framework:** XR Interaction Toolkit (XRI) & Unity New Input System
* **Target Hardware:** Meta Quest
* **3D Art & Texturing:** Autodesk Maya, Substance 3D Painter
* **UI & Typography:** TextMeshPro
* **Audio & Post-Processing:** Spatial Audio, URP Volume Profiles
